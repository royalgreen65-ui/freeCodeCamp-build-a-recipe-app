Simple Recipe

Recipe

This is a simple recipe for a delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, milk, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Pour the mixture into a pan and cook over medium heat.
  3. Stir occasionally until the mixture thickens.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy your meal.
